Who gets water in Pimpri-Chinchwad?

A ward-level look at drinking-water supply, built from RTI replies — and from how much of that reply refused to resolve into data.

What the RTI asked — and what came back

Three formal asks. The map below only holds scraps from the first. Red means we still lack a digitised, ward-joinable answer.

Ask 01

Ward-wise water distribution

  • Supply schedules for each ward — last five years
  • Daily allocation in MLD, and shortfalls
  • Planned versus actual water supply

What we have been able to gather

No full five-year ward matrix. No clean planned vs actual MLD table. At best, a few joinsable scraps (hours / informal schedule notes) — enough for one coloured ward per zone on the map. Everything else is grey.

Ask 02

Non-revenue water (NRW)

  • Records of leakages, theft, billing inefficiencies
  • Share of supply that is unaccounted for
  • Mitigation steps being taken

What we have been able to gather

No ward-wise NRW. No usable leakage / theft / billing split. No single city % we can publish with confidence from this reply set. This ask is essentially unanswered for the public map.

Ask 03

Infrastructure & cyber-physical systems

  • Operational and defunct pipelines + last maintenance
  • SCADA, IoT, or AI distribution systems + audits
  • How far water governance is digitised — and how security is ensured

What we have been able to gather

No public GIS layer of pipes to overlay on wards. No SCADA / IoT / AI audit packet we could open or link. Maintenance logs remain inside the bureau — not on this page.

Where PCMC's informal settlements stand

Not everyone in Pimpri-Chinchwad lives in a house with a registered address. This is PCMC's own list of 37 recognised slum settlements — where they are, how many homes they have, and who owns the land they sit on.

Land ownership matters here: settlements on corporation (मनपा) or government (सरकारी) land have a clearer path to services and rehabilitation. Settlements on private (बाजगी) land often don't — which affects whether they ever get piped water, drainage, or legal recognition at all.
